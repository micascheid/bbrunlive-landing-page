# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 19, 2026

BB Run Live ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- First name
- Last name
- Email address
- Password (stored securely in encrypted/hashed form)

If you sign in using Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from those services. Apple users may choose to hide their email address using Apple's private relay service.

### Feedback and Bug Reports
When you submit feedback or bug reports through the App, we collect:
- Your name
- Your email address
- The content of your feedback or report

### Automatically Collected Information
The App does not collect location data, device identifiers, or usage analytics beyond what Apple and Google may collect through their respective app stores and operating systems.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate your access to the App
- Respond to your feedback and bug reports
- Improve the App's functionality and user experience

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted database service, hosted in the United States (US-West region). We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Passwords are never stored in plain text and are cryptographically hashed.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Your information may be shared only in the following circumstances:
- **Service Providers:** We use Supabase to store and manage user data securely.
- **Authentication Providers:** If you use Google or Apple Sign-In, those services process your authentication according to their respective privacy policies.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

### Account Deletion
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the App:
1. Go to the "More" tab
2. Tap on your account information
3. Select "Delete Account"

Upon deletion, all your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems.

### Data Access
You may contact us to request access to the personal information we hold about you.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## 7. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to external websites (such as the Photo Contest page). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
