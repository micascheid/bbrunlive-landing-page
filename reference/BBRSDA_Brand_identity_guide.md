# BBRSDA Brand Identity Guide (Markdown Version)

## Overview
The Bristol Bay Regional Seafood Development Association (BBRSDA) brand represents quality, sustainability, and the value of the Bristol Bay fishery. This guide defines how the brand should be consistently expressed across digital and physical mediums.

---

## Brand Principles

- Reflect the strength and quality of Bristol Bay seafood
- Maintain a clean, modern, and professional aesthetic
- Ensure consistency across all touchpoints
- Prioritize clarity and readability

---

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo (2-Color)

**Preferred Usage:**
- Use the full-color primary logo whenever possible
- Designed to align with Bristol Bay Sockeye Salmon branding
- Should be the default in most applications

**Variants:**
- Stacked logo (icon above text)
- Horizontal logo (icon left of text)

---

### Small Size Logo

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Use when logo width is less than ~2 inches
- Ensures readability at small scales
- Maintain clear spacing and avoid crowding

**Variants:**
- Stacked small version
- Horizontal small version

---

### Secondary Logos (1-Color)

**Usage Cases:**
- When full color is not possible (e.g., print constraints, embossing)
- Minimal or single-color design environments

**Variants:**
- Monoline (outline style)
- Solid (filled style)
- Available in brand red or blue

---

## Logo Rules

### Do:
- Maintain consistent proportions
- Use approved color variations only
- Ensure sufficient spacing around the logo
- Place on backgrounds with strong contrast

### Don’t:
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Change colors outside the palette
- Add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)
- Place on visually noisy backgrounds

## Color Usage Guidelines

- Use **Red** for emphasis, calls to action, and highlights
- Use **Blue** as the primary brand anchor
- Use **Dark Blue** for strong contrast and backgrounds
- Use **Neutrals** for backgrounds and supporting UI

---

## Typography

### Primary Typeface (Logo Only)

**Brandon Grotesque**
- Used exclusively in the logo
- Some letterforms are customized
- Do not substitute or replicate for general use

---

### Secondary Typeface (All Other Use)

**Gotham**

**Usage:**
- Headlines
- Body copy
- Digital and print materials

**Guidelines:**
- Maintain clean, modern hierarchy
- Avoid overly decorative fonts
- Use consistent weights for structure

---

## Typography Hierarchy (Recommended)

- **H1:** Gotham Bold
- **H2:** Gotham Medium
- **Body:** Gotham Book / Regular
- **Captions:** Gotham Light

---

## Imagery & Style Direction

- Emphasize freshness, quality, and authenticity
- Use high-contrast, clean photography
- Highlight seafood (especially salmon) in a premium way
- Avoid cluttered or overly stylized visuals

---

## Layout Principles

- Keep layouts clean and spacious
- Use strong alignment and grid systems
- Maintain visual hierarchy through typography and color
- Avoid overuse of elements — simplicity is key

---

## Digital Application Guidance

### UI / Product Design

- Primary buttons: BBRSDA Red
- Backgrounds: Stone or White
- Text: Dark Blue or Black equivalents
- Accents: Blue tones

### Accessibility

- Ensure strong color contrast
- Maintain readability at all sizes
- Avoid relying solely on color to convey meaning

---

## Summary

This brand identity is built on:
- Simplicity
- Consistency
- Strong color usage
- Clean typography
- High-quality imagery

All implementations should reinforce the value and integrity of the Bristol Bay fishery.
